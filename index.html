What does Neuroflow actually do?+

Neuroflow is an AI decision layer: it brings together the inputs your team already has, finds the patterns that matter, and turns them into clear, shareable next steps.

Will it work with our existing tools?+

Yes. The workspace is designed to start with simple uploads and expand into connected sources as your team builds confidence.

How do you handle sensitive data?+

Your workspace is permissioned by design. We keep explanations close to outputs, so teams can understand what shaped a recommendation.

Can I change plans later?+

Absolutely. Start small, then add teammates, workflows, and connectors as your operating rhythm grows.
